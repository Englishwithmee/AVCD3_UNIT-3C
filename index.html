Step 03 · grammar theory

Past Continuous & Past Simple

Use the past continuous to describe an action in progress at a particular moment in the past.

01 · Past Continuous

I was sleeping at 7 p.m.
They were riding their bikes.

(+) Subject + was/were + V-ing

(−) Subject + wasn't/weren't + V-ing

(?) Was/Were + subject + V-ing?

Was: I, he, she, it
Were: you, we, they

🇻🇳 Diễn tả hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ.

02 · Past Simple

His bike broke down.
Alastair’s beard froze.

Use the past simple for a completed action or event.

🇻🇳 Diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra và kết thúc trong quá khứ.

Spelling: study → studied; stop → stopped; break → broke.

03 · Interrupted action

Past continuous (long/background action) + when + past simple (short action).

I was cycling when my phone rang.
Ongoing
Event

🇻🇳 Một hành động ngắn xen vào hành động đang diễn ra.

04 · Two actions in progress

Use while with past continuous for two simultaneous activities.

I was studying while my sister was watching TV.

when often introduces the shorter event; while often introduces the ongoing action.

🇻🇳 Hai hành động đang diễn ra đồng thời trong quá khứ.

Quick comparison

When: I was having breakfast when I heard the news.

While: While he was riding through Siberia, his beard froze.

At a specific time: At 8 p.m. yesterday, we were doing our homework.

Step 05 · speaking production

What were you doing?

Use each time marker or sentence starter to create your own sentence. Then tell your partner what happened.

Speaking patterns
At + time, I was + V-ing ...
I was + V-ing ... when + S + past simple ...
While I was + V-ing ..., someone was + V-ing ...

Pair Work · Ask and answer

Student A: What were you doing at 9 p.m. last night?

Student B: I was ... when ...

Student A: What was your family doing while you were ...?

Extension: Ask a follow-up question using What happened next?
